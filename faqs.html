Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common queries about our school, programs, and admissions.

SCROLL
What is the admission process? +

You can apply online or visit the school office. Our admissions team guides you through document submission, interaction, and enrollment.

Do you offer scholarships? +

Yes, need-based and merit-based scholarships are available. Eligible students can apply during admission.

What extracurriculars are available? +

Sports, arts, music, dance, debate, science club, and various student clubs.

Is transportation available? +

Yes, safe GPS-enabled school bus services with trained attendants cover major routes.

What are the school timings? +

9:30 AM to 3:30 PM, Sunday to Friday. Pre-school timings may vary.

What age groups do you accept? +

Ages 3 to 16 years for preschool, primary, and secondary levels.

What is the student-teacher ratio? +

We maintain a low ratio of 15:1 for personalized attention.

Do you provide meals? +

Yes, healthy snacks and meals prepared in a hygienic kitchen are provided.

How can parents stay updated? +

Via school app, emails, newsletters, and regular parent-teacher meetings.
