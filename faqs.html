Helpful Information

Everything You Need to Know

Explore answers to some of the most common questions from parents and students. For additional information, our school administration is always available to assist.

What is the admission process? +

Admissions are available for eligible students from Nursery to Grade 9, subject to seat availability. Parents can contact or visit the school office to learn about the application process, required documents and enrollment procedures.

Which grades does Kinder Garden Academy offer? +

Shree Kinder Garden Academy provides education from Nursery through Grade 10, supporting students across their early childhood, primary and secondary learning stages.

What are the school timings? +

School timings may vary according to the academic schedule and class level. Parents are encouraged to contact the school office for the current timetable, including arrival and dismissal times.

Does the school offer scholarships? +

For information about available scholarships, eligibility requirements and application procedures, please contact the school administration directly.

What extracurricular activities are available? +

Students have opportunities to participate in activities beyond classroom learning, including sports, creative activities and school events. Contact the school for details about current activities and programs.

Is school transportation available? +

Parents can contact the school office to confirm current transportation availability, routes and service arrangements. Our administration can provide the latest information.

What is the medium of instruction? +

Kinder Garden Academy follows an English-medium approach to teaching and learning, helping students develop their communication skills alongside academic knowledge.

How can parents stay informed about their child's progress? +

Parents can stay connected with teachers and the school administration through school communication and parent-teacher interactions. Contact the school to learn about its current communication arrangements.

How can I contact Kinder Garden Academy? +

You can visit Shree Kinder Garden Academy at Sanfebagar-03, Achham, Nepal. You can also email us at info@kindergardenacademy.edu.np or use the Contact Us page on our website.
