Learning at Every Stage

Education from Early Years to Grade 10

Kinder Garden Academy provides a learning journey from early childhood through primary and secondary education. Our academic approach encourages students to develop knowledge, confidence, creativity and responsible values at every stage of their school life.

Age 3–5

Pre-School Program

Our Pre-School nurtures young minds through play-based exploration. Children develop social skills, creativity and early literacy in a warm, stimulating learning environment.

  • Play-based learning and sensory activities
  • Phonics, numbers and early literacy
  • Creative arts and music activities
Meet Our Teachers
Grades 1–5

Primary School

Our Primary School program builds strong academic foundations with a focus on literacy, numeracy and critical thinking. Students are encouraged to explore new ideas, develop essential skills and build a lifelong love of learning.

  • STEM and project-based learning
  • Language development and communication
  • Sports and creative arts activities

Grades 6–10

Secondary School

Our Secondary School program prepares students for further education and future opportunities through academic learning, independent thinking, leadership development and practical knowledge.

  • Science, technology and ICT learning
  • Personal development and leadership activities
  • Academic assessment and examination preparation
